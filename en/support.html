Support

Write to us at support@savut.eu. A real person will answer, in English or Italian.

If something goes wrong, it helps a lot to know which app, which iPhone, iPad or Mac you use, the iOS or macOS version and what you were doing. If you are testing a beta, you can also send feedback with a screenshot from TestFlight.

suns

For suns, also tell us the audio interface or mixer connected, how many channels you were recording and in which format.

Where are my recordings?

In the Files app, in the suns folder on your device (on a Mac, in Music › suns): one folder per project, a subfolder per take, one WAV file per channel.

Which audio interfaces work?

Class-compliant USB ones, which work on iPhone, iPad and Mac without drivers: from small 2-input interfaces to digital mixers with 18, 32 or more channels (up to 48).

Can I record with the screen locked?

Yes: recording carries on with the screen locked and in Low Power Mode. If a call comes in or an alarm goes off, suns closes the files and pauses; when the call ends it starts again by itself with a new take (you can turn this off in Tips and help › Avoid Interruptions).

What does the loudness meter measure?

Loudness to ITU-R BS.1770, in LUFS: momentary (0.4 s) and short-term (3 s). The LED shows the sample peak in dBFS. The full guide is in the app, under Impostazioni › Consigli e aiuto › Come si legge (Settings › Tips and help › How to read it).
