In the room, people see only what you want them to.

cine is a media player for running live events.

One cue list, one output. Video, images and audio, fired when you need them.

Mac only for now. [ almost ready ]

01why it exists

When you have to put a video or a graphic on air, there are usually three ways to go.

  • Presentation software: you get no control.
  • A general-purpose player: you never feel at home in its menus, and sooner or later the desktop shows up in the room.
  • Professional software: beautiful and complete, but built for people who only do that. If it isn't your trade, you can't make head or tail of it.

There's a gap in the middle, and everyone fills it by making do with what they already know.

cine sits right there: simple, but built not to fail.

02two things, always

  • The output shows only what you want. No desktop, no menus, no windows. If the second screen's cable drops, the output puts itself back when it returns.
  • You always know what is on air, and you always see what is coming next. You check it before sending it, then press GO.

This is where I spent most of my time.

03two scenes, one request

at the village fair

A fixed background and three videos to fire when you're asked. You're not in the trade and you don't want to be: you just need it to work.

at the conference or the show

Thousands of people in the room, a rigid cue list, no margin for error. You're in the trade, and you need just one thing: for it to work.

If it works for one, it works for the other.

04try it

cine is almost ready and the first version is running in a small group. If you have an event to try it on, or you just want to see it before everyone else, write to me: support@savut.eu
