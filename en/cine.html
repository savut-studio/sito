cine

A media player for live events.

Video, images and audio in a cue list. Press GO and the next one plays.

[ early beta ]

01a few tastes

  • One cue on air at a time: cine is a player, not VJ software.
  • Preview → GO → On air: you see and hear the next one before sending it out.
  • List or tiles: same cues, two ways to fire them (even with the show running).
  • For each cue you decide what happens at the end: it stops, holds, the next one starts, it loops.
  • The second screen is locked down: if the cable drops, the output comes back by itself.
  • The B key takes everything to black.

Mac only for now. External control (OSC, MIDI) isn't there yet.
