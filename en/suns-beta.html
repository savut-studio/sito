Welcome to the suns beta.

It's free (it would be odd otherwise, it's a beta). I ask one thing only: use it on a real job and tell me how it went, even in two lines. What doesn't work, what you're missing, what made you swear.

The beta is open to 50 people. If the link no longer works, the places are gone: write to me anyway.

While it's in beta, don't use it as your only recording of the event of your life. Always keep a copy on something else.

01how to install

  1. Download TestFlight from the App Store, on iPhone, iPad or Mac. It's made by Apple and it's free.
  2. From the device you want suns on, tap the button below.
  3. In TestFlight tap Install.

02what I've tested it with

I've tried it with old and new phones, an iPad and various computers. And with mixers and interfaces such as Behringer X32, XR18, Wing and UMC202HD, Yamaha TF-Rack…

Class-compliant USB interfaces and mixers (the ones that work without drivers) should all work. "Should", precisely: there are too many combinations to try them all on my own. I'd love to have endless devices and peripherals, but first you need to make me rich.

It's compatible with iOS, iPadOS and macOS 26, but most of the testing was done on 27.

Have you tried it with your own interface or mixer? Tell me: device, interface, how many channels and how long it lasted. I need it to know what works and what doesn't.

If you also send me a photo of suns at work, a desk, a stage, your backpack, even better. Tell me if I can put it here: I publish nothing without your permission, and if you want I'll blur the faces.

03how to report a problem

From the app: Settings › Report a Problem. It prepares an email with the app's event log, which helps me understand what happened. Or write to support@savut.eu.

To help me, tell me:

  • which device and which system you use
  • which interface or mixer, and how many channels
  • what you were doing when it happened
  • whether it happened once or keeps happening

04technical notes

  • Channels: 1 to 48 (I set the limit myself).
  • Sample rate: the interface's own, with no conversion. So far tested up to 48 kHz.
  • Formats: 24-bit WAV (the default, the safest), 16-bit WAV, 24-bit ALAC (lossless), AAC at 256 and 128 kbps. AAC goes up to 48 kHz: above that, suns records in 24-bit WAV.
  • Files: one mono file per channel. The WAVs are Broadcast WAV, with start date and time, and beyond 4 GB they become RF64. Markers are inside the WAV and in a .csv file next to it.
  • Where they end up: in the Files app on iPhone and iPad, in Music › suns on the Mac.
  • Measurements: loudness ITU-R BS.1770 (LUFS M over 400 ms, LUFS ST over 3 s), sample peak in dBFS.
