Who we are.

Truth is, for now the "we" is just me.

My name is Alex, I live in Friuli, in north-east Italy, and since the early 2000s I've worked as an audio and video technician: live broadcasts, filming, installations, conferences, shows, post-production and so on.

«Al vûl savût par fâ scliçots.»

It's a saying in Friulian. It's a play on words: to make peashooters, the scliçots, you need elder, the savût. But savût also means "known": besides the elder, you have to know how to make them. The tool and the trade, both. The name comes from there.

I'm not a developer. The code is written by Claude Code, and I guide it patiently: I decide what the app should do, I test it in the field and I send it back when it doesn't work.

For anything, even a criticism: support@savut.eu. I answer myself.
