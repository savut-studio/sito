vôs

An audio editor for spoken word, on the Mac.

Made for podcasts and multi-voice interviews.

[ early beta ]

01a few tastes

  • Level: brings the voices to the same volume, but only where someone is speaking: the noise in the pauses doesn't rise.
  • Clean up: finds where a voice is silent and lowers or cuts the bleed from the other microphones.
  • Move a cut and see what moves with it, lane by lane.
  • Close-up cut: double-click a cut and nudge it in 10 ms steps.
  • A volume line on every clip: select a stretch, drag it down, and you've made a duck.
  • Coming from REAPER? Import the edit and carry on here.

Mac only for now, and there's no export yet.
